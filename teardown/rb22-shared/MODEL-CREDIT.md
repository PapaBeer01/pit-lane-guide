# RB22 3D model

"2026 Redbull RB22" (https://skfb.ly/pOvxI) by Dave Love on Sketchfab, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credited on the teardown page.

Changes made for The Pit Lane: wheels (tyres, rims, discs) split into four parts centred on their axles and the
steering wheel into its own part, so they can turn; meshes compressed (meshopt) and textures resized to 1024 px WebP
(37.5 MB → 3.7 MB). Script: scratch `split.mjs` + `gltf-transform optimize --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024`.
